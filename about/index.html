What is a full stack data dev?

We jokingly coined the term “full stack data dev” (FSDD) in 2024 since the industry’s growing expectation for data scientists is mirroring those of a front end developer to become full stack developers.

Gone are the days of a data scientist only needing deep expertise in ML model development. Increasingly, data scientists are expected to demonstrate epxertise in data engineering, data governenace, data analytics, data modelling, product experimentation, ML ops, AI engineering… The list keeps growing by the year!
